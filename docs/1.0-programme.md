# 1.0 Programme

How the work on the applications is organised, in a page. The **programme** is
the organisation that owns, operates, maintains and enhances the applications;
an **application** is the thing itself, its code and its environments
([1.3](1.3-workstreams.md#programme-and-application)). Roles are
[1.7](1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md). The detail is in [1.2](1.2-change-lifecycle.md) (how a change reaches
production), [1.3](1.3-workstreams.md) (who owns what) and
[1.4](1.4-programme-activities.md) (the recurring work); the rules on one page
are [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).

## Who does what

The owner decides; Claude does the work and brings anything needing a
decision to him. Claude alone decides in Process Definition, Programme Tooling
and Delivery Tooling, and for tooling and documentation changes anywhere. The
owner decides anything reaching production, the product and its rules, and
anything irreversible outside git ([1.7](1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md#who-decides)).

## Workstreams

Workstreams each own a part of what the programme maintains and a named set of
artefacts, and never finish. They group into the application, running it,
delivering it and the programme itself. The generic ones, and how an
application adopts them, are [1.3](1.3-workstreams.md); each application lists
its own in its workstream register, `3.n.4` in [2.1](../records/2.1-applications.md),
and the programme's own are [2.6](../records/2.6-workstreams.md).

## How a change is made

A **requirement** says what should be true. Triage places it in a workstream;
a **project** gathers related requirements so they are designed together, and
delivers them in **work packages**, one delivery each, by one **route**: a
release of the application, a change to the repository, or something applied
by hand. A release is tested on preview and rehearsal, where the application has them, before it ships. The
steps, rules and commands are [1.2](1.2-change-lifecycle.md) and
each application's release procedure (Tile Lite Elite's is
[4.3.3](https://github.com/delphside/tile-lite-elite/blob/main/docs/4.3.3-testing-ci-and-release.md)).

Everything is tracked in GitHub: issues typed Requirement, Project or
Decision, their fields, and the project board. The daily state is read with
`scripts/programme/board/board-status.py`,
`scripts/programme/board/board-inbox.py` and
`scripts/programme/board/board-actions.py`.

## Recurring work and improvement

Work that never closes is **programme BAU**: sweeping the board, the capacity
plan, the benchmark run, and the weekly digest, each with a frequency in
[1.4](1.4-programme-activities.md). The weekly digest measures where the owner's
time goes and tracks lessons, recurring problems and improvement initiatives;
Claude reviews it weekly and the owner monthly.

## Where things are recorded

| what | where |
| --- | --- |
| what should be true, and why it was decided | the issues |
| what is true now | these documents |
| what changed, and why | the commits |
| what shipped, and when | each application's delivery log, `3.n.1` |
| what exists outside git | each application's artefact register, `3.n.2` |
| periodic reports | the weekly digests in [`records/2.4-weekly-digest/`](../records/2.4-weekly-digest/), and each application's capacity plans, `3.n.3` |
| the applications, their records and their numbers | [2.1](../records/2.1-applications.md) |
