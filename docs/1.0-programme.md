# 1.0 Programme

How the work on the applications is organised, in a page. The **programme** is
the organisation that owns, operates, maintains and enhances the applications;
an **application** is the thing itself, its code and its environments
([1.3](1.3-workstreams.md#programme-and-application)). The rules on one page
are [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).

## Who does what

**The owner decides anything reaching production, the product and its rules,
and anything irreversible outside git, in every workstream.** Claude does the
work and brings anything needing a decision to him. Elsewhere Claude decides
alone in the areas
[1.7 *Who decides*](1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md#who-decides)
lists; the roles are [1.7](1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md).

## Workstreams

Workstreams each own a part of what the programme maintains and a named set of
artefacts, and are permanent. The generic ones, how they group and how an
application adopts them are [1.3](1.3-workstreams.md). Each application lists
its own in its workstream register, `3.n.4` in
[2.1](../records/2.1-applications.md), and the programme's own are
[2.6](../records/2.6-workstreams.md).

## How a change is made

A **requirement** says what should be true.

1. Claude triages it into a workstream, with the owner except where
   [1.7 *Who decides*](1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md#who-decides) says
   Claude triages alone.
2. A **project** gathers related requirements so they are designed together.
3. The project delivers them in **work packages**, one delivery each, each by
   one **route**: a release of the application, a change to the repository or
   something applied by hand.
4. A release is tested on preview and rehearsal, where the application has
   them, before it ships.

The steps, rules and commands are [1.2](1.2-change-lifecycle.md) and each
application's release procedure (Tile Lite Elite's is
[4.3.3](https://github.com/delphside/tile-lite-elite/blob/main/docs/4.3.3-testing-ci-and-release.md)).

Everything is tracked in GitHub: issues typed Requirement, Project or Decision
(and Ticket, in the service desk), their fields, and the project board. Claude
reads the daily state with `scripts/board/board-status.py`, `board-inbox.py`
and `board-actions.py` ([1.1 *The board*](1.1-programme-tooling.md#the-board)).

## Recurring work and improvement

Ongoing work is *programme business as usual* (BAU): the activities in
[1.4 *The register*](1.4-programme-activities.md#the-register), each with its
frequency. The weekly digest measures where the owner's time goes and tracks
lessons, recurring problems and improvement initiatives; Claude reviews it
weekly and the owner monthly.

## Where things are recorded

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| what should be true, and why it was decided | the issues |
| what is true now | these documents |
| what changed, and why | the commits |
| what shipped, and when | each application's delivery log, `3.n.1` |
| what exists outside git | each application's artefact register, `3.n.2` |
| periodic reports | the weekly digests in [`records/2.4-weekly-digest/`](../records/2.4-weekly-digest/), and each application's capacity plans, `3.n.3` |
| the applications, their records and their numbers | [2.1](../records/2.1-applications.md) |
| recurring problems and improvement initiatives | [2.7](../records/2.7-improvement-registers.md) |
| requests, incidents and complaints from players and other users | Tickets in the private service desk ([1.8](1.8-service-desk.md)) |
