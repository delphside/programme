# 3.3.5 Infrastructure role holders

Who holds the roles for Infrastructure that the programme-wide record,
[2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md), leaves to each application; 2.2 holds the
rest, the owner among them. The roles are defined in
[1.7](../../docs/1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md); the form is the
[role-assignment template](../../docs/templates/role-assignment.md). Workstream
names are those of [3.3.4](3.3.4-workstreams.md).

## Who holds each role here

| Role | Held by |
| --- | --- |
| design authority | the service owner, in the next table |
| release manager, environments manager | the service owner |
| security | policy: the programme authority, in [2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md); the hosts' controls: the service owner |

| Owner thread | Design authority for | Kind | Runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| service owner | Operations & Infrastructure, Capacity & Performance: one thread across every application ([1.3](../../docs/1.3-workstreams.md#generic-workstreams), [3.1.5](../tile-lite-elite/3.1.5-roles.md)) | permanent | local, on the owner's machine |
