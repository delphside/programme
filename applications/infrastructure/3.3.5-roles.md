# 3.3.5 Infrastructure role holders

Who holds the roles for Infrastructure that the programme-wide record,
[2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md), does not hold; the owner is among those 2.2
holds. The roles are defined in
[1.7](../../docs/1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md); the form is
[the template](../../docs/templates/role-assignment.md). Workstream names are
those of [3.3.4](3.3.4-workstreams.md).

## Who holds each role here

| Role | Held by |
| --- | --- |
| design authority | the owner thread below; Process Definition and Programme Tooling are the programme authority's, in [2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md) |
| release manager, environments manager | the service owner, locally: a host change is a delivery by date, applied by hand |
| security | policy: the programme authority, in [2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md); the hosts' controls: the service owner |

| Owner thread | Design authority for | Kind | Runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| service owner | Operations & Infrastructure, Capacity & Performance | permanent | local, on the owner's machine |
