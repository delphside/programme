# 3.2.2 Dark Portal artefacts

Dark Portal's artefact register holds two things:

- its artefacts by route
- everything under change control that leaves no trace in git

This file is the only record of the second, so each entry carries enough to
recreate it. It has no such entry yet: what exists or is planned is under *Not
yet recorded*, where each entry says who records it.

## Artefacts by route

The rule is [1.2 §2.15](../../docs/1.2-change-lifecycle.md#route); this is its
answer here.

| Route | Dark Portal's artefacts |
| --- | --- |
| Production Release | none yet: the image of the *collector* (the program that fetches the auction house data) and whatever its deliveries install on its hosts |
| Application Tooling | `sizing/`, the scripts that build a synthetic year of data and time the collection, the hourly comparison and the analysis queries; build, deploy and operate scripts: none yet |
| Docs and Programme Tooling | the `5.x` documents changed on their own, and anything else in `delphside/dark-portal` |
| Other | its own Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) resources (under *Not yet recorded*); its GitHub settings: none yet. Maintenance of its hosts is [Infrastructure's](../infrastructure/3.3.2-artefacts.md), a delivery numbered by date ([1.2 §2.15](../../docs/1.2-change-lifecycle.md#host-maintenance-is-the-infrastructure-applications)) |

## The hosts

Dark Portal has two hosts of its own: `dark-portal`, its production VM, and
`dark-portal-nonprod`, its non-production VM. Infrastructure records both, with
what they run on and the Oracle account they sit in, in
[its artefact register](../infrastructure/3.3.2-artefacts.md), and
[6.3.4](https://github.com/delphside/infrastructure/blob/main/docs/6.3.4-hosts.md)
says how they are reached and that an address is not recorded. A path on a
host is written as
[1.2 §2.13](../../docs/1.2-change-lifecycle.md#213-the-impacted-artefacts-and-why-they-are-listed-early)
says, its role then the path, such as `non-production:/srv/dp`.

## Not yet recorded

Known to exist or planned, and not yet described. The issue
delphside/dark-portal#14 *#11 WP A Del 1 of 2: Non-production VM and image
store* records its own artefacts as it completes, and each other entry says who
records it:

- **The image store**: the two private registry repositories
  `dark-portal/candidates` and `dark-portal/releases`.
- **The bucket `dp-bronze`**: the bucket of *bronze* data (the source
  snapshots), which production writes and non-production reads.
- **Access to the image store and the bucket**:
  - the dynamic groups `dp-nonprod` and `dp-prod`
  - the Oracle user `dp-releaser` in the group `dp-releasers`, with its API key
  - the policy `dark-portal-storage`
  - on `dark-portal-nonprod`, the Linux users `dp-e1` to `dp-e6` and
    `dp-release`, and the directory `non-production:/srv/dp`
- **The non-production Blizzard API client**: the client used on
  `dark-portal-nonprod`, with its credentials in the file
  `non-production:/srv/dp/e5/blizzard.env`. Claude records it at the next
  change that touches it. The register records the file's path, never its
  contents.
- **The collector, its credentials and its data** (planned): recorded by
  delphside/dark-portal#7 *#5 WP B Del 2 of 5: consumable profiles* when the
  collector is deployed.
- **Backups, and keeping collection running through reboots and patching**
  (planned): recorded by delphside/dark-portal#7 when they are designed with
  the collector.
