# 3.2.5 Dark Portal role holders

Who holds the roles for Dark Portal that the programme-wide record,
[2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md), leaves to each application; 2.2 holds the
rest, the owner among them. The roles are defined in
[1.7](../../docs/1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md); the form is the
[role-assignment template](../../docs/templates/role-assignment.md). Workstream
names are those of [3.2.4](3.2.4-workstreams.md).

## Who holds each role here

| Role | Held by |
| --- | --- |
| design authority | the owner threads in the next table, each for the workstreams listed; the service owner, in [2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md), for Operations & Infrastructure and Capacity & Performance |
| release manager, environments manager | the service owner; [delphside/dark-portal#11](https://github.com/delphside/dark-portal/issues/11) will set the release path |
| security | controls: the service owner, including the Blizzard API credentials ([3.2.4](3.2.4-workstreams.md)) and its host's controls ([3.3.5](../infrastructure/3.3.5-roles.md)) |

| Owner thread | Design authority for | Kind | Runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Portal authority | DP: Application Architecture, DP: Market Analysis, DP: Client, DP: Delivery Tooling | permanent | cloud |

A busy area splits out as an on-call authority, as
[1.7](../../docs/1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md#permanent-and-on-call-authorities)
says.
