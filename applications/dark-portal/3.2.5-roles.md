# 3.2.5 Dark Portal role holders

Who holds the roles for Dark Portal that the programme-wide record,
[2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md), does not hold; the owner is among those 2.2
holds. The roles are defined in
[1.7](../../docs/1.7-roles-and-responsibilities.md); the form is
[the template](../../docs/templates/role-assignment.md). Workstream names are
those of [3.2.4](3.2.4-workstreams.md).

## Who holds each role here

| Role | Held by |
| --- | --- |
| design authority | the owner threads below, each for the workstreams listed; Process Definition and Programme Tooling are the programme authority's, in [2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md) |
| release manager, environments manager | the service owner, locally; the release path itself is [dp#11](https://github.com/delphside/dark-portal/issues/11)'s |
| security | policy: the programme authority ([2.2](../../records/2.2-roles.md)); controls: none here ([3.2.4](3.2.4-workstreams.md)); the host's: Infrastructure's ([3.3.5](../infrastructure/3.3.5-roles.md)) |

| Owner thread | Design authority for | Kind | Runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Portal authority | DP: Application Architecture, DP: Market Analysis, DP: Client, DP: Delivery Tooling | permanent | cloud |
| service owner | Operations & Infrastructure, Capacity & Performance: one thread across every application ([1.3](../../docs/1.3-workstreams.md#generic-workstreams), [3.1.5](../tile-lite-elite/3.1.5-roles.md)) | permanent | local, on the owner's machine |

The demand manager splits an on-call authority out of the Dark Portal
authority when one of its workstreams gets busy.
