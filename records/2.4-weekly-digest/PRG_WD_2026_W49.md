From the review-record thread of 2026-11-23, a lesson from two threads
changing one document at once:

- #252 *Review record: a check on the pull request, and three more forms
  read* and the W48 document review both changed the root README and 1.1.
  The review merged first; #252's rebase was settled hunk by hunk, which kept
  text the review had just rewritten. Two rules followed, agreed by the owner
  the same day: a branch whose text `main` has changed redoes its change on
  the new text and has the parts that came out different reviewed again, and
  a session force-pushes, with a lease, only a branch of its own (#255 *Rules:
  redo a change on main's new text; force-push the session branch with a
  lease*).

  will change on its pull request's `Claims:` line, `board-overlap.py` lists
  open pull requests that change or name the same file, and the weekly
  activities that change documents start one at a time (#256 *Claims: name
  the files a change will touch*).
