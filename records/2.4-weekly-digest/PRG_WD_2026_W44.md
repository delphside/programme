  review the rule rather than discover the inputs.

  findings by round, truth and reader together: 6, 6, 4, 3, 2, 0. Rounds 2 to

- **#71's update channels took five review rounds (2026-10-20).** Material
  findings by round, truth and reader together: several, 3, 4, 2, 0. Each
  fix round's decisions closed one gap and exposed the next case nobody had
  walked: the wire form for each new message, a bot holding two seats in one
  game, a deleted game's seats, start-up, then who feeds each bot object.
  None was a wording slip; each was a lifecycle case. *Proposed conclusion:*
  before the first round of a design that adds a subscription or a list, walk
  its lifecycle in the handover (create, start-up, add, remove, delete, two
  of the same) and have the maker answer each, so the reviewers check
  answers rather than find the cases.
