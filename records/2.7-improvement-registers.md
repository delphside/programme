# 2.7 Improvement registers

The programme's problem register and continual improvement register, which the
weekly digest reports. Claude updates them at the weekly review, and the owner
reviews them monthly. How a row is raised and moved is in
[1.4 *Continual improvement*](../docs/1.4-programme-activities.md#continual-improvement).
